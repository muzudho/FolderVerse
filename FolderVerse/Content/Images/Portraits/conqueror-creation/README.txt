2026-10-10. See selected-basic-versions.json and the original images, gallery
and balance notes under art-source/portraits/conqueror-balanced.

individual adjustments.

Adjust each portrait separately to retain the face, hands and characteristic toy.

area. Preserve aspect ratio. Keep original artwork under art-source.

portraits only; it preserves previous game tiles under art-source.
