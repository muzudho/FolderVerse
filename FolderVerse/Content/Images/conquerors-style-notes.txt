20 portraits, 5 columns x 4 rows; preserve identities, ordering, costumes, toys and poses.
Clothes: molded plastic thickness, soft color palette, bold black outer silhouette and fine interior lines; broad dark hard-edged cel shadows around half visible area, restrained gloss gradients on lit planes.
Faces: light skin with white-sclera contrast, preserve darker skin identities. Narrow irises with fine ink rims. Large white circular upper-right catchlight and small lower-left catchlight. Fine curled upper lashes, shorter ultra-fine lower lashes and one outer-corner upward flick.
Eye height assignments by row (N normal, S half-height slender, L approx 1.3x):
N L S L S
S L N S L
S N S L N
L N S L N
Hair: thin ink bangs; pure-white horizontal highlight ribbons with aligned upper/lower arcs following spherical volume. Bang and hat cast shadows; chin-to-neck shadow with intentionally bright collars.
Hair depth: only narrow boundary accents at overlaps behind neck, shoulders, arms or dark clothes. Original hair color dominates. Light hair uses pale atmospheric accents; dark hair near face uses nighttime dark tones and reflected light; distant dark hair against dark clothes uses narrow pale separation accents. Never fade whole rear hair or obscure long hair length.
Toys: bold black outer silhouette only; interior forms defined by rendered shading, material colors, contact shadows and gloss rather than pen lines.
